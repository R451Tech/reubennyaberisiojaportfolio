About

ICT · Software · Data, based on the Kenyan coast

A results-driven ICT & software professional with 7+ years across government, hospitality, education and manufacturing.

I'm Reuben Nyaberi (Sioja), a developer and operations professional based in Mombasa, Kenya, along the Kilifi coast. I run CheckME Solutions, building production software for the Kenyan market: rental management, retail, school and college systems, while also working on data analysis, database management and enterprise systems administration.

Alongside building products, I'm an ICT Officer with Kenya's Ministry of Education and have hands-on experience in hospitality systems (Opera, Fidelio, Cloudbeds), POS platforms, and remote technical support. I don't treat these as separate lanes, a good product needs the software built right, the data read correctly, and the infrastructure underneath kept reliable. That's the work I do.
